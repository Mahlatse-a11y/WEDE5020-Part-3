Weekly Hair Tips

Tip 1: Deep Conditioning

Use a deep conditioner once a week to keep hair hydrated and prevent breakage. Leave it in for 20 minutes under a shower cap.

Tip 2: Heat Protection

Always spray heat protectant before blow-drying or straightening. It reduces damage by up to 50%.

Tip 3: Scalp Massage

Massage your scalp for 5 minutes daily with oil to boost blood circulation and hair growth.
